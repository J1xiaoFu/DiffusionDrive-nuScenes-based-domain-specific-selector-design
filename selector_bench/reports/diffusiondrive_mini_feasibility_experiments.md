# DiffusionDrive Mini Feasibility Experiments

Last updated: 2026-07-06

Validation boundary: synthetic anchors are pipeline-smoke assets only. They are not used for feasibility conclusions or metric claims.

## Validation/Evaluation Policy

Clean validation is currently defined as standalone checkpoint evaluation through `/root/autodl-tmp/domain_selector/DiffusionDrive/tools/test.py` in a fresh eval work dir, with `--cfg-options work_dir=<fresh_eval_dir>` and raw outputs saved via `--out`. This avoids the train-time MMCV `EvalHook` + `TextLoggerHook` path that previously raised `KeyError: 'data_time'` after eval. The tested wrapper is `/root/autodl-tmp/domain_selector/selector_bench/scripts/18_eval_diffusiondrive_checkpoint.py`.

The standalone policy was tested on real nuScenes mini val info with real mini anchors and exited cleanly for the baseline, selector, and random checkpoints below. These mini validation readouts are useful for pipeline feasibility and small-scale sanity comparison only; they are not final model-performance evidence.
